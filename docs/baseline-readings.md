## What This Shows So Far

The 13 favorites span a wide range of implied win probabilities, from the **Saints at 52.8%** (a near-coin-flip game) up to the **Rams at 89.5%** (a heavy favorite). That spread matters for the study: a team already priced near 90% has little room left to move, while a team near 50% has the most room in either direction. The evening reading will be compared against these numbers to measure how much each team's price shifted over the day.
